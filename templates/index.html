        Methodology

        How PhishGuard AI reaches a risk score.

        1. Read the URL text onlyThe link is split into parts such as the domain, path and symbols. The website is never opened.
        2. Check for warning signsLength, extra dashes, odd characters, many subdomains and words like "login" or "verify".
        3. Compare with known websitesThe domain is measured against a list of trusted brands to catch lookalikes.
        4. Score with a machine learning modelThe signals are combined into a phishing score from 0 to 100.
        5. Explain the resultEach score comes with the reasons behind it.
        Everything runs locally. Scores are a guide, not a final verdict.
